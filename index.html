    How Chaos Dice! works

    This is a six-sided die with faces A–F, and every face starts at 0. The numbers don't stay put.

    1. Roll. The die lands with one face on top. That face's number is your result.
    2. Then the chaos. The face you rolled gains +1, and the face opposite it loses 1. Opposites are A↔F, B↔E and C↔D.
    3. Repeat. Over many rolls, faces drift far above or below zero.

    Blue numbers are positive, and bluer means higher. Red numbers are negative, and redder means lower. Black is zero.

    Your fate is sealed

    Your character's name is the seed. The same name always gives the same rolls in the same order. Capitals and extra spaces don't matter. Progress is saved on this device.

    Playing on another device, or lost your save? Enter the same name and your roll count, and your die is rebuilt exactly as you left it. It runs on the honour system.

    Controls

    Tap the die, press Roll, or hit Space. The speaker button turns sound on and off. Reset clears your saved character from this device.
