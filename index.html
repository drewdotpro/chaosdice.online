    How it works

    It's a six-sided die with faces labelled A to F. Every face starts at 0, but they don't stay there.

    1. Roll. Whichever face lands on top, its number is your result.
    2. The die changes. That face goes up by 1 and the face opposite it goes down by 1. The opposite pairs are A and F, B and E, and C and D.
    3. Keep rolling. Over time some faces climb high and others sink below zero.

    Blue numbers are positive: the bluer, the higher. Red numbers are negative: the redder, the lower. Black is zero.

    Same name, same rolls

    Your character's name decides every roll in advance, so the same name always gets the same results in the same order. Capitals and extra spaces don't matter. Your progress saves on this device.

    On a new device, or lost your save? Enter the same name and your roll count, and your die comes back exactly as you left it. We trust you not to fudge the count.

    Controls

    Tap the die, press Roll or hit Space. The speaker button turns sound on or off. Start over clears your character from this device.
